🔧 Stored Procedures (5 Tasks)

	sp_GetUserOrderSummary – Return total orders and amount for a specific user

	sp_AddNewProduct – Insert a new product with validation

	sp_UpdateUserStatus – Change status for users meeting specific conditions

	sp_ArchiveOldOrders – Move orders older than a year to an archive table

	sp_ResetInactiveUserStatus – Reset status of users inactive for 6+ months

--------------------

⚡ Triggers (5 Tasks)

	trg_BeforeUserInsert_CheckAge – Prevent users under 18 from being inserted

	trg_AfterOrderInsert_LogOrder – Log order details into an audit table

	trg_BeforeProductUpdate_ValidateStock – Prevent negative stock updates

	trg_BeforeUserInsert_NormalizeUsername – Convert usernames to lowercase

	trg_BeforeOrderDelete_BlockIfItemsExist – Prevent deletion if order has items


--------------------

🔍 Views (5 Tasks)

	vw_UserOrderCount – Show each user with total order count and amount

	vw_ProductSalesSummary – Display total quantity sold per product

	vw_ActiveUsersWithOrders – Users who are active and have at least one order

	vw_MonthlySalesByProduct – Monthly sales grouped by product

	vw_UsersWithNoOrders – Users who never placed any order

	csr_NotifyInactiveUsers – Loop through inactive users to trigger notification

	csr_RecalculateOrderTotals – Recalculate totals if product prices have changed
